Subscribe in Apple Calendar View or download the feed

Subscribe once on your Mac

  1. Choose File → New Calendar Subscription in Calendar.
  2. Paste this site’s calendar URL if it is not filled automatically.
  3. Choose iCloud for Location so it also appears on your iPhone.
  4. Set Auto-refresh to every hour or every day.

What makes this useful

Open an event to see the actual lineup, price, venue, schedule, practical notes, official links, competing events, and any details that are still TBD. Calendar updates arrive through the same subscription URL.

Reality check: Big Bear organizers change schedules. Each entry says when it was last checked and links back to its official source.

This is an independent, curated calendar—not an official City of Big Bear Lake or Visit Big Bear publication.
